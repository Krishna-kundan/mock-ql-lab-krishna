# Assessor

**Slug:** assessor
**Introduced by:** ptsf-lab

Internal PTSF staff member in a satellite office. Works regional and office review queues in the console, approves subsidies with amount guidance, and chats with patients. Never sees medical history.

## Access

- **Profile:** to decide
- **Permission set group:** to decide
- **Permission sets:** PTSF_Assessor_User (grants no Medical History access); others to decide
